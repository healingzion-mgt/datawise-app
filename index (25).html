🔒 Privacy

DataWise only stores what you log yourself — your subscription details, data entries and airtime entries. We never read other apps on your device or access your real carrier balance; numbers you see as "estimated" come from your own entries, not a live network connection.

❓ Help

Log your usage right after you notice it (e.g. after a big download) for the most accurate dashboard. Set a budget so DataWise can warn you before you run low.
